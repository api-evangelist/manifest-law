# Manifest Law (ManifestOS)

> Manifest Law operates ManifestOS, a communication and case management platform for the legal industry. It is backed by Menlo Ventures. Programmatic access is gated by a standards-based OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect authorization server at app.manifestlaw.com. This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the company's public discovery documents; no OpenAPI specification was published at probe time.

## Product
- ManifestOS: https://app.manifestlaw.com (communication and case management)
- Company site: https://manifestlaw.com
